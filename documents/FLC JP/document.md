Prospective Product Opportunities

These opportunities include but are not limited to:

with trading venues

▪ ETP S-Box: Based on the FIX Orchestra technology, a new standard from the FIX Protocol organization, ETP S-Box enables 
stock exchanges and tier-1 brokers to design, manage, organize, and publish their trading APIs and protocol specifications 
for their members and clients, delivering tangible and substantial process improvements, increased member engagement, 
and reducing the risk of trading mistakes. 

▪ ETP GEMS SaaS and Enterprise: Scalable, highly-performing exchange simulation platform, with a WebSocket front-end, 
cloud back-end, extensive support for trading flows, and modular architecture supporting FIX and binary exchanges that 
can be used for regression and performance testing

▪ Production exchange line handlers: Existing line handlers supporting exchange-specific FIX and binary protocols can be 

cheaply repurposed as production line handlers

▪

Fidessa testing (hosted and enterprise): Esprow's ETP Studio is the only application supporting the Fidessa OpenAccess 
protocol and capable of carrying out end-to-end regression testing of Fidessa systems

▪ ETP GSP – FIX Venue Simulation-as-a-Service: ETP GSP venue-specific high-fidelity FIX exchanges simulators could be 

offered to the entire market

▪ Enhanced Professional Services: Testing consultants can be bundled with Esprow's ETP applications to provide higher 

value solutions that include tooling and implementation

▪ Portfolio Growth: A large organization would realize greater growth for the portfolio of existing ETP applications than 

Esprow could alone

Esprow Solution

▪ Historically the testing process was carried out manually

▪ Often outsourced to offshore companies specializing in 
manual testing, often taking months and having delays

▪ Constrained budgets and the advent of DevOps and 

Agile/Continuous Integration management practices have 
pushed the market to seek adoption of automated testing 
strategies that can be integrated within the product 
development and delivery pipeline

▪ This trend is set to continue and accelerate because, as 
financial institutions become more reliant than ever on 
technology, being able to react to market changes quickly 
has become more of a requirement than a competitive 
advantage

▪ Clients also pay exchanges for order flow and algo testing, 

with limited time windows and high costs

▪ Esprow’s product portfolio delivers key automation 

functionality in the last step of clients’ technology delivery 
chain – testing of their business-critical technology

▪ A key competitive advantage for Esprow has been its ability 
to broaden the scope of testing that can be carried out 
with its application

▪ This is achieved by supporting more trading protocols, both 
client and exchange simulation, system virtualization, add-
on functionality to manage test results, and also by 
fulfilling the requirements of operational departments, 
such as auditing and compliance

▪ Increasing demand for a SaaS solution to provide market 
simulation for order flow testing and algo testing and to 
become independent from exchange-provided testing
